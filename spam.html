Anti-Spam Policy

Last updated: [Date]

We are committed to providing a safe, secure, and spam-free environment for our users. This Anti-Spam Policy outlines our strict zero-tolerance approach to spam and sets forth the rules governing communications related to our website and services.

1. What is Spam?

Spam, also known as junk email, is unsolicited bulk commercial email. In the context of our website, spam also includes unsolicited comments, forum posts, direct messages, and any other form of unauthorized mass communication.

2. Zero-Tolerance Policy

We maintain a strict zero-tolerance policy against spam. We do not engage in spamming, nor do we authorize any third parties to send spam on our behalf. Any account found to be associated with the transmission of spam will be immediately suspended or terminated without prior notice.

3. User Obligations

By using our website and services, you agree that you will not:

  • Harvest or collect email addresses or other contact information of other users from the service by electronic or other means for the purposes of sending unsolicited emails or other unsolicited communications.
  • Use our forms, comment sections, or messaging systems to distribute unauthorized advertising, promotional materials, "junk mail," "spam," "chain letters," or "pyramid schemes."
  • Send emails to our users that contain false, misleading, or deceptive information in the subject line or body.
  • Attempt to bypass our automated spam filtering systems.

4. Automated Spam Filtering

Our messaging systems automatically scan all incoming emails and internal messages, and filter out those that appear to be spam. However, no message filtering system is 100% accurate. From time to time, legitimate messages may be filtered out by our systems. If you believe this has happened to a message you have sent, please contact us by another means.

5. Reporting Spam

If you suspect that you have received spam from our domain or from an account registered on our website, please report it to us immediately. Please forward the suspected email or provide a link to the offending content along with a brief explanation.

Report spam to: legal@csqlabs.com

6. Changes to this Policy

We may amend this anti-spam policy at any time by publishing a new version on this website. It is your responsibility to review this policy periodically for any changes.
